Company

A company run by agents and operated by people

Akshi means eye. Agents do the work here, and a person keeps watch over all of it.

01 / What Akshi is

The back office for firms that sign their work

Accountants, lawyers and trademark agents in India spend most of their staff's hours on the same steps: collect the records, match them, calculate, draft. Akshi's agents do those steps, and the professional who signs keeps the judgement and the responsibility.

Every workflow we build adds to one shared engine for matching evidence, labelling facts, checking citations and recording who approved what.

02 / The aim

Agents in every function, people accountable for all of them

Akshi is designed so that agents do the day-to-day work in every function: research, engineering, delivery, sales and finance.

People set direction, approve whatever leaves the company and answer for the result. An agent cannot hold that responsibility, so a named person always does.

03 / Why

A firm that sells agent-run work should run on it

We would rather find the failures in our own operations than have a client find them in theirs. Running the company this way shows us where agents are reliable, where they need tests, and where a person has to decide.

04 / Rules

What we hold ourselves to

  • A named person approves anything that leaves Akshi.
  • Nothing is trusted until it has been tested against real past work.
  • No invented facts, in a client draft or on this website.
