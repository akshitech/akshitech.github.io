How it works

How a firm's documents become signed work

Every Akshi workflow, from a tax notice to a trademark objection, runs the same four steps. The firm keeps every decision that carries professional responsibility.

01 / The steps

Four steps, every time

  1. The firm sends the documents

    A notice, an examination report, invoices, returns, ledgers. Each client has its own workspace, and the original files are kept as the reference.

  2. Agents do the work

    They read the documents, split the work into its points, match the evidence, do the calculations and draft the output.

  3. Every fact is labelled

    Each statement shows whether a document proves it, the client only says so, it still needs proof, or nobody knows yet.

  4. A named professional signs

    A chartered accountant (CA), advocate or trademark agent at the firm reviews, corrects and approves the version that goes out. The firm files or sends it.

02 / Evidence

How facts are labelled

The reviewer sees at a glance which parts of a draft rest on paper and which do not.

  • Confirmed by documentsAn uploaded record supports it.
  • Confirmed by the client onlySomeone has said so, with no document yet.
  • Needs proofA possible explanation that is not yet supported.
  • UnknownNobody has established it either way.

Every total opens to the documents and the calculation behind it. If the records are incomplete, or cover a different period from the notice, the result says so and is never shown as final.

03 / Citations

Why every citation is checked before approval

In August 2026 the Gujarat High Court set aside a series of GST orders after finding that the officer had relied on AI-generated case law. Some of the judgments did not exist and others did not say what was claimed. The court warned that departing from the safeguards since issued by the Gujarat State Tax department to its officers, which require such citations to be verified against primary sources, would be treated as contempt.

The Supreme Court's draft rules on AI in courts, published in June 2026, would have lawyers tell the court when a pleading was prepared with AI.

A reply signed by your firm needs the same care. In Akshi a draft cannot be marked ready until every law and judgment it cites has been checked against the source.

Faiz Enterprise v. State Tax Officer, 2026:GUJHC:53490-DB, reported by Bar and Bench and LiveLaw. Draft rules reported by Bar and Bench.

04 / Limits

What Akshi does not do

  • Sign, file, pay or send anything. A professional at the firm does that.
  • Give legal or tax opinions of its own. Akshi prepares the work, and the professional advises the client.
  • Put a fact, law, judgment or attachment in a draft without a source the reviewer can open.
  • Take on work with no documents to check against, or no professional to sign it.

05 / Client data

Your clients' data stays your responsibility, and we treat it that way

A firm answers for its clients' records under its professional duty of confidentiality, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 adds further duties, most of them from May 2027 under the 2025 Rules. Using a vendor does not move either.

We act as your processor and sign a data processing agreement before any client file is uploaded. Ask us for the terms before you decide.

  • Storage. DigitalOcean, in Bengaluru.
  • Reading and drafting. Anthropic, through its Claude models.
  • Checking each draft against its sources. OpenAI.

We use both AI providers through their business services, which do not train models on what we send. Each data processing agreement names these sub-processors, where they process data and how long they keep it.

06 / How we charge

You pay for finished work

The price is agreed before we start, and set against what the work costs your firm today.

  • Recurring work. Per client, per month, for work such as monthly reconciliation.
  • Notices and disputes. Per case, from the notice through the reply, hearing and appeal.
  • A workflow of your own. A fixed fee for the review and the build, then per case or per month to run it.

07 / Akshi itself

Akshi runs on the same rules

Akshi means eye. Agents do the daily work in every function here: research, engineering, delivery, sales and finance. People set direction, approve whatever leaves the company and answer for the result, because an agent cannot hold that responsibility.

Running the company this way shows us where agents are reliable, where they need tests and where a person has to decide, before a client meets any of it.

  • A named person approves anything that leaves Akshi.

  • No invented facts, in a client draft or on this website.
