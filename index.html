01 / An example

A tax notice, start to finish

A shop owner gets a notice from the GST (goods and services tax) department. It says they claimed ₹4.8 lakh of tax credit they were not entitled to. They have 30 days to reply, and they hand the notice to their chartered accountant (CA).

  1. The firm uploads the notice and the records

    The notice, the purchase invoices and the government's record of what suppliers reported.

  2. Akshi's agents match them

    38 invoices match, covering ₹4.3 lakh. About ₹50,000 is not backed by any document yet, and two credit notes are missing.

  3. Akshi drafts the reply

    Point by point against the notice, with every fact linked to the document it came from.

  4. The CA checks and signs

    The CA chases the two credit notes, corrects what needs correcting and signs. The reply goes out on time, and the firm's staff did none of the matching.

Illustrative example, not client data.

03 / The record

Every piece of work shows how it was made

When a department, a court or a client asks how a reply was prepared, the firm can show them.

  1. Where each fact came from

    One of four labels on every statement, from proven by a document to unknown.

  2. How each number was reached

    Click any total to see its documents and its arithmetic.

  3. Which citations were checked

    Each law and judgment cited, checked against the source before sign-off.

  4. Who approved it

    The professional who approved each version, by name.
