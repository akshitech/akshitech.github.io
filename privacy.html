Privacy policy

What this website collects, what happens when you contact us, and how client files are handled.

Who we are

It covers this website, akshi.tech, and what you send us when you get in touch. Work we do for a firm under a signed agreement is governed by that agreement and its data processing agreement.

What this website collects

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no forms. If you choose Light or Dark in the footer, your browser remembers that choice on your device; it is never sent to us. Its fonts and scripts are served from akshi.tech itself, not from third parties.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

When you contact us

If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your name, your contact details and what you tell us. For this information we are the data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We use it only to reply and to discuss the work you ask about. We do not sell it or share it with anyone other than the services that carry and help answer it: Google Workspace for email, WhatsApp, run by Meta, for messages, and the AI providers named under Client data when our agents help prepare a reply.

We keep these conversations for 24 months after the last contact, then delete them, unless they become part of an engagement.

Client files

A firm sends us client documents only after we have both signed a data processing agreement. For those documents we act as the firm's data processor: we process them only on the firm's instructions and for the work it has asked for. The firm remains the data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and answers to its clients for them.

Client files are stored with DigitalOcean in Bengaluru, India, and processed by the AI providers named under Client data. How long they are kept is set out in each data processing agreement.

Where data is held

Google, Meta, GitHub, Anthropic and OpenAI may store or process data on servers outside India. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 allows this except to countries the Government of India restricts.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us:

  • for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and what we do with it;
  • to correct, complete or update it;
  • to erase it, where we no longer need it for the purpose it was given or the law no longer requires us to keep it;
  • to let a person you nominate exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot do so yourself.

Write to {{ site.data.contact.email }} with any request or complaint. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This website is meant for professional firms and is not directed at children.
