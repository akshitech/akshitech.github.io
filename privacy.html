What this website collects

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no forms. Its fonts and scripts are served from akshi.tech itself, not from third parties.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your name, your contact details and what you tell us. We use this only to reply and to discuss the work you ask about. We do not sell it or share it with anyone other than the services that carry the message: Google Workspace for email and WhatsApp, run by Meta, for messages.

Client files

For those documents we act as the firm's data processor: we process them only on the firm's instructions and for the work it has asked for. The firm remains the data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and answers to its clients for them.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us:

  • for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and what we do with it;
  • to correct, complete or update it;
  • to erase it, where we no longer need it for the purpose it was given or the law no longer requires us to keep it;
  • to let a person you nominate exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot do so yourself.
